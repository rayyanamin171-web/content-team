## Atlas's angle
Reem isn't glamorous like Saadiyat or Yas, so a "beautiful island" Reel won't work. What works is **one practical insight people can use: service charges.** Two Reem apartments can earn the same rent but leave you thousands of dirhams apart every year. That's the kind of advice that gets saved and shared, and it shows exactly why someone should talk to you before buying.

## Caption
Two apartments on Al Reem Island. Same size, same rent. One can leave you up to AED 20,000 a year worse off.

The difference is service charges, roughly AED 25 to 45 per sq ft a year depending on the tower. On 1,000 sq ft that gap compounds every single year.

Reem is part of ADGM, prices are up around 18% in a year, office leasing is surging, and entry prices are still among the lowest of Abu Dhabi's islands. Just check the building before you fall for the view.
