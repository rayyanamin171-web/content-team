## Atlas's angle
Fahid's story is location plus concept: **an island sitting between Yas and Saadiyat, designed as a "wellness island".** The honest point that builds trust is the price. Apartments launched at a big premium to the rest of Abu Dhabi, and handover is years away. Saying that openly separates you from every agent just pushing the launch.
