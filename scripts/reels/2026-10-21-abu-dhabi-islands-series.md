# Reel Series: "The Islands of Abu Dhabi" (Reels 1–7)

> Written by Atlas · 2026-10-21 · For Rayan Maniya (@rayyanmaniya)
> Goal: establish Rayan as the person who knows Abu Dhabi's islands better than anyone. Trust & authority first.
> Status: DRAFT. Numbers marked [VERIFY] come from agency and portal reports (Oct 2026 research). Confirm before filming. Sources are at the bottom.

## Atlas's notes before you film
- **Why a series works:** the same opening line and look on all 7 builds recognition. Use the same intro line every time: *"Island [X] of 6. Abu Dhabi, honestly."* People binge series and follow to catch the next one.
- **Order matters:** Reel 1 sets up the series. Al Maryah goes 5th, so it lands just before your YouTube video on branded residences and pushes viewers there.
- **Pushback:** don't make these tourist guides. Every Reel answers one question for an investor: *who is this island for, and who should avoid it?* That honesty is what makes HNIs trust you.
- **Format mix:** Reels 1, 4 and 7 talking to camera. Reels 2, 3, 5 and 6 voiceover over drone/B-roll with you appearing at the start and end.
- **Length:** 45–60 seconds each. That's enough for one real insight without losing people.
- **Visual identity:** same lower-third style, island name in big clean serif text, and a small "1/6" style counter.
