## Atlas's angle
Most Yas content sells the Disney headline. That's the trap. The real Yas story is **visitor demand**: tens of millions of visits a year create rental demand. The honest catch is that Yas also has the biggest pipeline of new homes in Abu Dhabi. So the message is: **Yas works, but only if you pick the right building.** That positions you as the person who knows which building, which makes it a natural lead-gen CTA.
