## Atlas's angle
Ramhan is the newest island, so there's little to film and lots of hype. The honest angle *is* the content: **"Here's what it is, and the questions I'd ask before buying on an island that doesn't exist yet."** Opening with Alabbar's name gets attention. The due-diligence questions build trust and lead naturally into the CTA.
