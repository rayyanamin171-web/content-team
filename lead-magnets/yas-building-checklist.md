# The Yas Island Building Checklist
### By Rayan Amin · Aeon & Trisl Real Estate · @rayyanmaniya

Yas Island has strong demand and a big pipeline of new homes. On Yas, the building you choose matters more than the island. Here's what I check before any client buys.

**1. Exact location on the island**
How close is it to what people come for: Yas Bay, Yas Mall, the theme parks, the beach, Yas Links? Walking distance to demand drivers protects rent.

**2. The view, now and later**
What's on the empty plot next door? Check the masterplan. A sea view today can be a wall in three years.

**3. Supply around it**
How many similar units are being handed over nearby in the same year? Your tenants will be choosing between them.

**4. Developer and handover record**
Has the developer delivered on time and to spec before? For off-plan, what happens to your payment plan if handover slips?

**5. Net yield, not gross**
Ask for the actual service charge per sq ft. Subtract it, plus management and vacancy, from the expected rent.

**6. Unit type that rents fastest**
Studios and 1-beds usually yield more. 2–3 beds attract longer-staying families. Match the unit to your goal.

**7. Short-term rental rules**
Is holiday letting allowed in this building? With Yas visitor numbers, that can change your return, but rules and fees vary.

**8. Exit**
Who buys this unit from you in 5 years? Resale depth matters as much as rental demand.

If a building passes all eight, it's worth a closer look.
