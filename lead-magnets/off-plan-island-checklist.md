# The Off-Plan Island Checklist
### By Rayan Amin · Aeon & Trisl Real Estate · @rayyanmaniya

Buying on an island that's still being built can be the best entry point or the most expensive lesson. These are the questions I go through with every client before they sign.

**1. Access and infrastructure**
When will the bridge, access roads and utilities actually be finished? Ask for the official timeline, not the sales estimate.

**2. Handover risk**
What's the handover date in the contract? What happens to your payment plan if it slips, and is there any compensation clause?

**3. Which phase you're buying**
Early phases often launch cheaper but carry more uncertainty. Later phases cost more, but you can see construction progress. Know which one you're in.

**4. Payment plan and escrow**
How much do you pay before handover? Is the money held in a regulated escrow account?

**5. Developer track record**
What has this developer delivered before, on time and to spec? Visit a completed project if you can.

**6. Service charges and running costs**
Island communities with beaches, marinas and private amenities often carry higher service charges. Get the estimate in writing.

**7. Golden Visa eligibility (for buyers abroad)**
Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa, subject to current rules. Check how off-plan and mortgaged purchases are treated at the time you buy.

**8. Exit plan**
Can you resell before handover? Is there a fee or a minimum payment threshold first?

If a project gives you clear answers to all eight, it deserves a serious look.
