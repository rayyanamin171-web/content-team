# Branded Residence Checklist
### By Rayan Maniya · Investment Advisor · @rayyanmaniya

Six questions I go through with every client before they buy a branded residence in Abu Dhabi or Dubai.

**1. What exactly does the brand deliver?**
List the actual services: concierge, housekeeping, valet, hotel facilities. Ask which are included in the service charge and which are paid extra.

**2. What is the service charge, and what is my net yield after it?**
Ask for the building's service charge budget per sq ft, not an estimate. Subtract it from the expected rent before judging the return.

**3. How long is the brand agreement, and what happens if it ends?**
The brand is there under a management or licence agreement. Find out the term, the renewal terms, and what happens to the building if the brand leaves.

**4. What are the rental rules?**
Is short-term rental allowed? Is there a mandatory rental programme? What fee does the operator take?

**5. Is the premium in line with real resale prices?**
Compare the asking price with actual transactions in completed branded towers nearby, not with launch prices.

**6. Who is the developer, and what have they delivered before?**
Check their track record on quality and handover dates, especially for off-plan.

If a project passes all six, the premium is probably buying you something real.
