## Hook Formulas, Rewritten for Dubai Real Estate
A formula is only a shape. Every hook must contain a **specific fact, number, place or story**.

**Curiosity**
- "I was wrong about [Dubai belief]." → "I was wrong about JVC."
- "Most bad Dubai deals come down to one thing: [surprising cause]."

**Story**
- "Last week a client asked me [question]."
- "I almost told a client to buy [X]. I'm glad I didn't."
- "Three years ago, [area] was [state]. Today, [state]." `[VERIFY]`

**Value**
- "How to [outcome] in Dubai without [common pain]."
- "3 things I check before any client buys off-plan."
- "Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead."

**Contrarian**
- "Unpopular opinion: [bold but defensible statement]."
- "[Common advice] costs foreign investors [specific result]."

## Sounding Human, Not AI
HNIs scroll past content that sounds generated. **Never write:**
- **Contrast reveals:** "It's not X, it's Y." State the point directly.
- **Negation lists:** "No X. No Y. No Z."
- **Self-answered questions:** "The result? 12% yield." Say it plainly.
- **Stock phrases:** "Unlock", "Take it to the next level", "Here's the thing", "Game-changer", "In today's fast-paced world"
- **Engagement bait:** "Agree?", "Let that sink in", "Read that again"
- **Manufactured vulnerability:** "I'll be honest, I was terrified..." without a real story
- **Emoji bullets** (🚀✅💡) and **em dashes** in captions
- **"Broetry":** one short line per sentence for the whole caption. Write like a person talks.

Allowed in moderation: one list of three per piece, one dramatic fragment per piece.

## Spoken Script Test
Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a client across a table in DIFC, rewrite it.
