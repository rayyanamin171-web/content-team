# Skill: Trust Psychology for HNI Audiences

> Source: principles selected from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `marketing-psychology` (MIT License), applied to our audience.
> Learned: 2026-10-21 · Used by: Atlas (and future strategy/sales agents)

**Guardrail:** we use psychology to make true things clear and memorable, never to manipulate. No fake scarcity, no fake urgency, no hidden catches. Our audience is sophisticated, and getting caught once ends the relationship.

## The Principles We Use
| Principle | What it means | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | People trust proven experts | Specific numbers, process detail, "what I see with clients". Show expertise rather than claim it. |
| **Pratfall effect** | Small admitted flaws make experts more likeable | "Dubai isn't right for everyone. Here's who shouldn't buy." Say where off-plan is a bad idea. |
| **Loss aversion** | Losses hurt about twice as much as gains feel good | Lead with avoidable mistakes ("the fee nobody mentions") more than gains |
| **Regret aversion** | People freeze when they fear a wrong decision | Give checklists and frameworks that make the decision feel safe |
| **Anchoring** | The first number sets the frame | Compare to London, Mumbai, Riyadh or Toronto prices first `[VERIFY]` |
| **Framing** | Same facts, different feeling | "AED 8K a month in rent pays most of the mortgage" vs a raw yield %. Must be accurate. |
| **Reciprocity** | Give first and people want to give back | Every piece delivers real value before any CTA |
| **Mere exposure** | Familiarity builds trust | Consistent posting, look, opening line and sign-off |
| **Liking / similarity** | People trust people like them | Your relocation story; anonymised client stories they recognise themselves in |
| **Zeigarnik (open loops)** | Unfinished things stick in the mind | Tease the payoff early and deliver it late |
| **Peak-end rule** | People remember the best moment and the ending | Put one standout insight mid-video, and make the ending strong rather than fading out |
| **Curse of knowledge** | Experts forget what beginners don't know | Explain "off-plan", "DLD fee" and "service charge" in one line the first time |
| **Survivorship bias** | Success stories hide failures | Call it out: "You only hear about the flips that worked." This builds trust. |

## Quick Check Before Publishing
1. Did we give value before asking?
2. Did we admit at least one limitation or risk?
3. Is every persuasive number true and sourced?
