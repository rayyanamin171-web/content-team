# Skill: YouTube Structure (12–20 min)

> Sources: ideas adapted from kkoppenhaver/cc-skills `youtube-producer` (no license file, so concepts only, rewritten in our own words) and ailabs-393/ai-labs-claude-skills `script-writer` (MIT).
> Learned: 2026-10-21 · Used by: Atlas

## 1. Nugget + Packaging (do this before writing a single line)
Every video has a **nugget**, the knowledge, wrapped in **packaging**, the problem, story or fear that makes someone click. The nugget alone is invisible and the packaging alone is clickbait. We need both.

| Nugget (what they learn) | Packaging (why they click) |
|---|---|
| Off-plan payment plans | "The off-plan mistake that traps foreign investors" |
| Service charges by area | "This Dubai apartment looks cheap. Here's what it really costs." |
| Golden Visa via property | "What AED 2M actually gets you in the UAE" `[VERIFY threshold]` |
| Rental yield by community | "Where I'd put AED 1.5M in Dubai right now" |

Packaging questions: What mistake does this prevent? What fear does it answer? What does the viewer secretly want to know? What do insiders know that outsiders don't?

**Start with the title and thumbnail, then write the video to deliver them.**

## 2. Title Patterns (premium versions)
- **Contrarian:** "Stop buying off-plan in Dubai (until you know this)"
- **Permission:** "You don't need AED 5M to invest in Dubai"
- **Question:** "Is Dubai real estate a bubble in 2027?"
- **Insider / POV:** "What I tell every investor before they buy in Dubai"
- **Specific number:** "I analysed 5 Dubai communities. Only 2 make sense."
- **Story:** "My client almost lost AED 400K. Here's why."

Parenthetical qualifiers add credibility: "(in 2027)", "(as a foreigner)", "(honestly)".
Banned: shocked faces, "INSANE", "you won't believe", fake urgency.

## 3. Thumbnail Rules (Iman Gadzhi-style premium)
- One dominant subject: a skyline, a villa, a number, or you
- **3 words max** on the image. The thumbnail asks the question and the title completes it. Never repeat the title on the thumbnail.
- You: calm and confident, slight smile or serious. No shock face.
- Clean, cinematic, high contrast, golden-hour Dubai tones

## 4. The First 30 Seconds
| Time | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Show proof of the title's promise. No "hi, welcome". | Drone shot of the tower + "This building has a problem nobody talks about." |
| 3–10s | Stakes: the problem they didn't know they had, plus an open loop | "If you bought here in 2024, you might be overpaying by 20%." `[VERIFY]` |
| 10–20s | Who it's for + one line of credibility | "If you're investing from abroad, this is for you. I advise investors on this every week." |
| 20–30s | Promise + roadmap (not the answer) | "By the end, you'll know exactly how to check any building before you buy. But first..." |

## 5. Three-Act Body
- **Act 1 (0–2 min): stakes.** The problem, a personal or client story, what's at risk.
- **Act 2 (60–70%): the journey.** 3–5 chapters of 2–3 minutes each. Alternate data, story and visuals. Include what went wrong. Mistakes build more trust than wins.
- **Act 3 (last 20%): payoff.** Deliver what the title promised, give the bigger lesson, circle back to the opening, then one CTA.

## 6. Retention Beats
- A **re-hook every 60–90 seconds**: "But here's where it gets interesting...", a reveal, or a question
- **Change the visual every 2–3 minutes**: talking head, B-roll, on-screen data, map, walk-through
- **Open loops**: tease the best insight early and pay it off late
- Breathing room after a complex point: a B-roll moment with no talking

## 7. One CTA Only
One action per video, framed as a benefit. Example: "If you want me to look at a property you're considering, the link's below." Never stack subscribe, like, comment and DM in one ending.

## 8. Cut Every YouTube Video Into 3–5 Reels
| Reel type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Hook Reel** | The most surprising moment, with no context. Caption: the question the video answers. |
| **Correction Reel** | "Stop doing X when buying in Dubai" + why, then tease the full answer |
| **Permission Reel** | "You don't need X to do Y": the simpler path |
| **Story Reel** | The client story or personal moment from the video |
| **Data Reel** | The one number that surprises people `[VERIFY]` |
