## Concept A (recommended): "PAYING FOR A LOGO?"
**Pattern:** L1 Face + Building, with the "doubt question" angle (competitor pattern #1, done calmly).
**Why:** it asks the exact question the cold open asks ("the most expensive logo you'll ever buy") without repeating the title. The title then answers it: "Worth the Premium? (Honest Breakdown)". No number, so nothing to verify.

Mock-up: `yt-concept-a.svg` → `yt-concept-a-1280.png`, `yt-concept-a-160.png` (placeholders, not final art).

## Concept B: "SAME ISLAND. 2X?"
**Pattern:** L4 Split Screen (competitor pattern #5 done cleaner: two options, one focal point).
Two towers side by side at the same time of day, thin vertical divider. Left: a standard Al Maryah tower, slightly cooler. Right: a branded tower with warm glow. "2X?" huge in the centre over the divider; "SAME ISLAND" small above it. Rayan optional, small, bottom-left.
**⚠️ Blocked until Market Research verifies the ~2x per-sqft gap.** If it's verified, this is the strongest concept, because it shows the cold open's hook literally. If not verified, drop "2X" and don't use it.

## Concept C: "ABU DHABI'S QUIET ISLAND"
**Pattern:** L3 Map Pin (competitor pattern #4, "overlooked Abu Dhabi").
Wide drone shot of Al Maryah at dusk, a thin gold outline around the island, Rayan small in the lower right third. Text "THE QUIET ISLAND" (3 words) top-left.
Softer and more aspirational. Weaker curiosity, but good as a later A/B option or as the Instagram cover.
