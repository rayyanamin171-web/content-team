Honourable mentions: **personal experience** ("I Bought a Dubai Apartment 18 Months Ago… Here's What Happened", Leader Of No One, 15.2K, 5.2x; "Do I Now Regret Buying 2x Properties…", Joe Butler, 7.1K, 2x) and **insider quote** ("Alabbar Just Said It…", Steven Leckie, 14.8K, 10x).

**Takeaway for Rayan:** the biggest multipliers in this niche are on **small channels** with a sharp question or risk. Production polish isn't what's winning; the promise is. That's good news for a new channel: a clean, premium thumbnail with an honest, sharp question can stand out.
