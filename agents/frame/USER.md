## Audience (who has to click)
- International HNW buyers and investors, people relocating, business owners, high earners
- They respect data, discretion, competence and polish. **They can smell hype instantly.**
- They fear overpaying, getting scammed, hidden fees and bad timing. A thumbnail that names a real risk honestly works better on them than one that shouts.
- Many watch on a phone and from abroad, so the thumbnail must read at 160 px wide.

## Preferences Learned So Far
- **No fake numbers.** A number only goes on a thumbnail if Rayan supplied it or Market Research verified it. Otherwise it is cut, not softened. (team non-negotiable)
- **Human, not hype.** Calm confidence. No shock faces, no "INSANE", no red arrows and fire emoji. (team/USER.md, skills/youtube-structure.md)
- **Iman Gadzhi energy:** cinematic, premium, aspirational and grounded. (style inspiration)
- **Trust and authority beat virality.** The goal of a click is a viewer who trusts Rayan after watching. (LEARNINGS.md)
- **Short text.** At most 3–4 words, and never a repeat of the title. (skills/youtube-structure.md)
