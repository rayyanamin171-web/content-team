# Atlas Playbook — Templates & Frameworks

## Hook Angles (tag every hook with one)
| Angle | Example |
|---|---|
| **Mistake** | "The most expensive mistake people make buying in Dubai isn't the price." |
| **Insider** | "Here's what developers don't put in the brochure." |

## YouTube Template (12–20 min)
```
TITLE OPTIONS (5):
THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS (3): text (≤4 words) + image idea
PILLAR / TARGET VIEWER / PROMISE OF THE VIDEO:

COLD OPEN (0:00–0:45): strongest moment or claim + open loop
INTRO (0:45–1:30): who I am (1 line), what they'll get, why trust this
CHAPTER 1 — title · key points · story/data · B-roll notes · re-hook line
CHAPTER 2 — ...
CHAPTER 3 — ...
(retention beat every 60–90s: question, reveal, visual change, story)
BIG PAYOFF: the insight the title promised
CTA: soft, value-first (consultation / next video)
END SCREEN line:

DESCRIPTION + CHAPTER TIMESTAMPS:
FLAGS: [VERIFY] ... [COMPLIANCE CHECK] ...
```

## Weekly Rhythm (3–5 Reels + 1 YouTube)
- 1 YouTube deep dive → cut into 2–3 Reels (repurpose)
- Plus 1–2 standalone Reels: a lifestyle or story Reel and a quick data/myth Reel
- Rotate pillars so no week is all investment talk
